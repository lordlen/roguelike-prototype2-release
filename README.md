# Controls

space - wait

z - swap (no turn cost)

x - reshuffle

c - defend

left click - move / attack

right click - examine (no turn cost)

# Explanation of combat

All characters have a deck consisting in the draw pile, discard pile, and hand. The hand can only hold 2 cards: primary and offhand. Primary is the active part of the card that is discarded upon used. Offhand is the passive, often reacting to an opponent's actions.

The most important action in combat is "attack". The attacker takes the attack value of its primary and deals that damage to the defender. The defender then takes the defense value of its offhand and subtracts it from the attack value. In this case, only the attacker's primary is discarded

Another action that can be taken is "defend". The defender takes the defense value of the primary card and adds that defense to their offhand for a whole turn. The primary is discarded when used.

Lining up your cards correctly is important. You may swap your offhand and primary to maximize damage and defense values for no turn cost. You may also choose to reshuffle your deck, putting your hand and discard pile back into the draw pile.
