# Controls

space - wait

z - swap (no turn cost)

x - reshuffle

c - defend

left click - move / attack

right click - examine (no turn cost)

# Explanation of combat

All characters have a deck consisting in the draw pile, discard pile, and hand. The hand can only hold 2 cards: main and off hands. Main hand is the active part of the card that is discarded upon used. Offhand is the passive, often reacting to an opponent's actions.

The most important action in combat is "attack". The attacker takes the attack value of its primary and deals that damage to the defender. The defender then takes the defense value of its offhand and subtracts it from the attack value. In this case, only the attacker's primary is discarded. While the off hand is not discarded, its defense value is halved each time the defender takes a hit.

Another action that can be taken is "block". While defending, the defense value of the offhand is not halved. Additionally, the off hand gains bonus defense, either undoing all of the halving or if it hasn't been halved, increases the defense by 50%. The off hand then is discarded at the start of your turn if 1. the defender is attacked while defending and 2. it has an on-block effect.

Lining up your cards correctly is important. You may swap your offhand and primary to maximize damage and defense values for no turn cost. You may also choose to reshuffle your deck, putting your discard pile back into the draw pile. The hand is kept unless an innate card exists, in which case gets added to the hand.
